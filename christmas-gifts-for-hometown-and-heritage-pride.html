More Christmas Gifts for Hobby Lovers: Hometown & Heritage Pride

This is part 3 of our Christmas hobby guide, for people who never stop talking about where they come from. The hometown section celebrates US states and regions from Alaska to Rhode Island, plus Canadian provinces filed with hometown pride, and places like the Adirondacks and Long Island. The heritage section covers families with roots farther afield, from Brazil, Lebanon and Ethiopia to the Lake District, Galway, Queensland, Tasmania and the Cotswolds. Each pairs a soft holiday character with a pun like “Merry Tennessee-mas” or “Merry Lake District-mas.”

Hometown designs shine on a hoodie for the trip back home, a sticker for the car or a card tucked into a care package for someone who moved away. Heritage picks make thoughtful gifts for grandparents, cousins abroad or anyone who lights up when their roots are seen.

Shopping for a foodie instead? Food and drink designs live on part 2, linked below, and music, games, crafts and outdoors hobbies are on part 1. Check the size chart on the listing before ordering clothing. Every item is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order early in the holiday season.
