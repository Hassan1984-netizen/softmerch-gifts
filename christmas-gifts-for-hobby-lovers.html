Christmas Gifts for Hobby Lovers: Music, Games, Crafts & Food

The easiest way to make a gift feel personal is to aim it at the thing they love doing most. This is our biggest guide, so it is split into sections: music and performing, from violins and saxophones to bagpipes and marching band; games, books and mindful hobbies, including chess, board games and meditation; crafts and making, like crochet, sewing, pottery and cross stitch; food and drink, from kimchi and pierogi to guacamole and mimosas; outdoors, garden and cars; and hometown and heritage pride, from Tennessee to the Philippines. Each pairs a soft holiday character with a pun drawn from the hobby, like “Merry Hookmas” or “Merry Plantmas.”

Match the product to where the hobby happens. Stickers suit an instrument case, game box, laptop or water bottle, mugs keep crafters and readers company, and a tee or hoodie is great for band practice, game night or a day in the garage. A greeting card with their favorite pastime is a cheerful extra on top of supplies or a class.

If you are ordering clothing, check the size chart on the listing. Each item is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order early in the holiday season.
