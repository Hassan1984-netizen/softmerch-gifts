Christmas Gifts for Hobby Lovers: Music, Games, Crafts & Garden

The easiest way to make a gift feel personal is to aim it at the thing they love doing most. This guide covers hobbies you can play, make or grow, split into sections: music and performing, from violins, fiddles and handbells to bagpipes and marching band; games, books and mindful hobbies, including chess, sudoku, board games and meditation; crafts and making, like crochet, needlepoint, macrame, weaving, pottery and calligraphy; and outdoors, nature and garage hobbies, from bonsai and terrariums to aquariums, birdwatching and classic cars. Each pairs a soft holiday character with a pun drawn from the hobby, like “Merry Hookmas” or “Merry Plantmas.”

Match the product to where the hobby happens. Stickers suit an instrument case, game box, laptop or water bottle, mugs keep crafters and readers company, and a tee or hoodie is great for band practice, game night or a day in the garage. A greeting card with their favorite pastime is a cheerful extra on top of supplies or a class.

Shopping for a foodie, or for someone proud of their home state or heritage? Food and drink designs live on part 2, and hometown and heritage pride designs live on part 3, both linked below. If you are ordering clothing, check the size chart on the listing. Each item is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order early in the holiday season.
