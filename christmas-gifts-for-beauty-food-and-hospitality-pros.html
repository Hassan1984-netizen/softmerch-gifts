Christmas Gifts for Hairstylists, Baristas, Chefs & Service Pros

December is the busiest month for anyone who makes other people look good, eat well or get where they are going. This guide is for them: hairstylists, barbers, nail techs, estheticians, tattoo artists, dog groomers, baristas, bartenders, servers, chefs, bakers, florists, photographers, flight attendants and wedding planners. Every design pairs a cheerful holiday character with a pun from their trade, like “Merry Fade-mas” or “Merry Brew-mas.”

A good gift for a service pro is a thank-you they can use at work. A sticker fits on a station mirror, knife roll case or water bottle, a mug suits the person who makes everyone else’s coffee, and a soft tee or hoodie is nice for the commute after a double shift. A greeting card with their design, tucked in with a tip, is a lovely way to thank a favorite stylist or barista, and salon, café or kitchen teams can pick one design for everyone.

Check the size chart on the listing before ordering clothing. Each item is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order early enough to hand it over at their last appointment or shift before Christmas.
