Halloween Gifts for Coworkers: Office & Creative Pros

Not every office goes all out for Halloween, but a cute design is an easy way to join in without a full costume. This page gathers our spooky-season picks for desk jobs and creative careers: accountants, lawyers, architects, graphic designers, photographers, software developers, data analysts, insurance agents, realtors, social workers, wedding planners and DJs. Each one pairs a friendly ghost or pumpkin with a work-specific pun like “Spreadsheets & Spells” or “Legally Spooky.”

A good office gift is low-key and useful. Stickers are made for laptops, monitors and water bottles, a mug brings October to the desk, and a tee works for a casual Friday or a team Halloween lunch. A greeting card with their design is a friendly note for a coworker’s birthday or work anniversary in spooky season, and a set of stickers is an easy treat for a whole team.
