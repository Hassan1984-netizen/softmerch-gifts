Christmas Gifts for Animal Lovers: Small Pets, Wildlife & Sea Life

Not every beloved animal barks or meows. This page is for people with rabbits, guinea pigs, chinchillas, bearded dragons, axolotls, tortoises, iguanas, cockatiels, koi and betta fish, and for anyone who adores bigger or wilder animals: horses, kangaroos, lions, pandas, monkeys, bison, elk, skunks, moles, porcupines, platypuses, crows, butterflies, blue jays and hummingbirds, plus ocean favorites like orcas, walruses, manatees, seahorses and jellyfish. Vets and bee lovers have their own picks too. Most designs pair a cute holiday take on the animal with a pun like “Merry Wheekmas” or “Merry Orca-mas.”

Gifts for less common pets and favorite wild animals are hard to find, so a design that shows their exact animal is a genuinely thoughtful surprise. Stickers suit a water bottle, laptop or supply bin, mugs are good company for early feeding routines or birdwatching mornings, and tees and hoodies are great for aquarium trips and nature walks. A card with their animal shows you remembered.

For clothing, check the size chart on the listing first. Every piece is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order well ahead of Christmas.
