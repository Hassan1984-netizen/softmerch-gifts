Christmas Gifts for Cat Lovers: Cute Cat Mom & Cat Dad Ideas

Every cat owner knows the Christmas tree is really just a very tall cat toy. These designs celebrate that chaos with breed and coat-color picks: Maine coons, Scottish folds, Bengals, Persians, tabbies, calicos, grey cats, white cats and the famously one-brain-celled orange cat, plus general gifts for cat moms and cat dads. Most pair a soft kawaii cat with a pun like “Meowy Fluffmas” or “Merry Orange-mas,” and there are everyday picks too, like a noodle-slurping cat, a kitten tangled in yarn and a sleepy cat who refuses to get up.

The best gift for a cat lover is one that looks like their cat, so match the coat first. A sticker is ideal for a laptop or water bottle (and safely out of paw’s reach), a mug suits the person who drinks coffee with a cat on their lap, and a soft tee or hoodie gives them something cozy to collect fur on. A greeting card with their cat’s look makes a cute holiday note from the cat itself.

Check the size chart on the listing before choosing clothing. Each item is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order with a little extra time before Christmas, especially if you are shipping to a friend.
