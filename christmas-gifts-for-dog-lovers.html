Christmas Gifts for Dog Lovers: Cute Dog Mom & Dog Dad Ideas

Dog people want gifts that look like their dog, not just any dog. This collection gathers our breed-specific Christmas designs, from corgis, dachshunds and Frenchies to goldendoodles, Maltipoos, Bernedoodles, Cane Corsos, Belgian Malinois, Great Pyrenees, Staffies, kelpies, border terriers, Irish setters, Vizslas, Italian greyhounds and bloodhounds, plus picks for rescue dog families, dog moms, dog dads, groomers and dog walkers. Most come with a playful pun like “Merry Wiener-mas” or “Merry Snore-mas,” and a few year-round favorites round it out: a golden retriever wrapped in Christmas lights, border collie, Aussie and beagle portraits, and a hug-your-hound corgi.

Start with the breed, then think about where they spend time with their dog. A sticker looks great on a water bottle, laptop or car window and is a perfect stocking stuffer, a mug suits slow mornings before the walk, and a hoodie or tee is made for the dog park. A greeting card with their dog’s breed is a sweet way to send holiday wishes from the whole household, pets included.

Check the size chart on the listing before buying clothing, or choose a sticker, mug or card when you are not sure. Designs are printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order early in the season if it needs to be under the tree by Christmas.
