Thanksgiving & Fall Gifts: Cozy Hostess and Thank-You Ideas

Thanksgiving is the in-between holiday that rarely gets its own gifts, which is exactly why a small, cozy one stands out. This page gathers our fall and Thanksgiving designs: a cute dinner host, a proud canned cranberry fan, a happy Canadian Thanksgiving turkey, a sweater-weather dachshund and a pumpkin-spice orange cat. There are also “thankful” designs for nurse practitioners, vets, chiropractors, dietitians, nail techs, paraprofessionals, librarians, hikers, plant lovers and corgi moms.

These make easy hostess gifts and warm thank-yous. A mug is a natural pick to bring to dinner, a cozy hoodie or tee suits crisp autumn weekends, and a sticker adds a fall touch to a water bottle or laptop. A greeting card with a thankful design is a lovely way to thank a nurse, teacher or neighbor this season.
