Christmas Gifts for Firefighters, Police, EMTs & Tradespeople

Someone has to answer the call, clear the road or restore the power while everyone else is opening presents. These Christmas gifts for first responders and tradespeople are for firefighters, police officers, sheriff’s deputies, EMTs, paramedics and 911 dispatchers, plus electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs, linemen, welders, mechanics, oilfield workers, heavy equipment operators, truck and delivery drivers, mail carriers, farmers and lumberjacks. Each design mixes a friendly holiday character with a pun from the job, like “Merry Volt-mas” or “Merry Flame-mas,” and the fire truck, dump truck and excavator designs delight little truck fans too.

People in these jobs like gifts that get used hard. A sticker can go on a hard hat, toolbox, lunch cooler or water bottle, a big mug rides along in the truck, and a hoodie or tee is easy to wear on a day off. For a station or crew gift exchange, one sticker each keeps things simple and fair, and a card is a kind thank-you for someone working the holiday shift.
