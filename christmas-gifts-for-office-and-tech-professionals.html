Funny Christmas Gifts for Coworkers: Office & Tech Pros

Office gift exchanges can be awkward, and a pun about someone’s actual job beats another scented candle. These Christmas gifts for coworkers are written for professionals: accountants closing the books, lawyers, project managers, software engineers, architects, cybersecurity pros, financial advisors, real estate agents and pastors. There are also two all-purpose picks, one for your favorite coworker and one for a boss worth thanking. Each pairs a soft holiday character with a line such as “Merry Ledger-mas” or “Merry Code-mas.”

For a Secret Santa or white elephant swap, small and useful wins. A laptop sticker is a quick, personal pick, a mug lives on the desk all winter, and a tee or hoodie works for casual Fridays and holiday parties. A greeting card is a thoughtful way to thank a manager or mentor without making it a big production, and stickers are an easy way to give one small thing to everyone on the team.

If you are not sure of a coworker’s size, skip clothing and choose a sticker, mug or card. Everything is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order before your office’s gift exchange date, not just before Christmas.
