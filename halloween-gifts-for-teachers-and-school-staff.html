Halloween Gifts for Teachers & School Staff: Cute Spooky Ideas

October at school means costume parades, candy negotiations and a lot of extra energy in every hallway. These Halloween designs are for the people who keep it all running: classroom and daycare teachers, special education teams, paraprofessionals, school counselors and school psychologists, ABA therapists, librarians and bus drivers. Each design pairs a friendly ghost or pumpkin with a school-specific line such as “Spooky Para Squad” or “Teaching Is Spooktacular.”

Teachers love a gift they can actually wear to school. A tee is perfect for staff spirit days and costume-free Halloween parties, and matching tees are a fun pick for a grade-level team. Stickers make sweet little treats from students and parents and look great on a laptop or water bottle, a mug helps with the post-party sugar crash, and a greeting card from the class is a kind way to say thanks.
