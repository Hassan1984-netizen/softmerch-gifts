Christmas Gifts for Nurses 2026: Cute & Funny Nurse Gift Ideas

Looking for nurse gifts that feel personal? These gifts for nurses cover RN, CNA, ICU and ER gift ideas, from small stocking stuffers to a Secret Santa pick for the unit, all with a cute holiday pun the night shift will get.

Nurses, techs and therapists often work straight through the holidays, so the best Christmas gifts for nurses are small, cheerful and easy to use on shift. This guide is for the whole care team: nurses and nurse practitioners, CNAs and medical assistants, physical, occupational and speech therapists, pharmacists, sonographers, radiology techs and vets. Each design pairs a soft holiday character with a pun from their own specialty, such as “Merry Rx-mas” or “Merry Xray-mas,” so it feels personal rather than generic.

A good gift here is practical with a wink. A sticker for a water bottle or laptop holds up on a busy unit, a mug earns its spot in the break room, and a cozy hoodie is welcome after a long night. Tees suit holiday spirit days, and a greeting card with their design makes a sweet add-on to treats for the whole unit.

For tees and hoodies, check the size chart on the listing; if you are not sure of their size, a sticker, mug or card is the safe choice. Everything is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates for your address and leave plenty of time before Christmas, especially for team gifts.
