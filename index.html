Soft Merch Gift Guide

Shopping for someone specific is easier when the gift already speaks their language. The Soft Merch Gift Guide sorts our original kawaii designs into 22 small guides by job, family role, pet, sport and hobby, so you can go straight to the nurse, the corgi mom, the pickleball fanatic or the new grandma on your list. Every design pairs a soft, friendly character with a pun written for one kind of person, which makes even a small gift feel chosen rather than grabbed.

Start with the person, not the product. Pick the guide that matches their job, their family title, their pet or the thing they love doing most, then scroll until a design makes you think of them. Each guide also explains which products suit that person best. Stickers are easy stocking stuffers and look great on water bottles and laptops, mugs earn a daily spot on the desk or in the break room, tees and hoodies are perfect for spirit days and cozy weekends, and greeting cards make a sweet add-on to any present.

For clothing, check the size chart on the listing before you order; if you are unsure of their size, a sticker, mug or card is the safe choice. Every design is printed to order on Redbubble, which also handles checkout and delivery, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates for your address and order early if a gift needs to arrive before Christmas.
