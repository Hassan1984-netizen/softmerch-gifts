Christmas Gifts for Athletes, Coaches & Sports Lovers

Whatever their sport, there is probably a pun for it here. This guide gathers our Christmas designs for athletes, coaches and weekend players: football fans, runners, cyclists, BMX riders, rock climbers, swimmers, scuba divers, surfers, skiers, snowboarders, figure skaters, disc golfers, riders, archers, karate and taekwondo students, pickleball, padel, badminton and table tennis players, volleyball, softball, lacrosse, rugby and cricket fans, wrestlers, boxers, gymnasts, cheerleaders, curlers, anglers and ice anglers, darts players, referees and racing fans, plus young basketball and soccer players. Most pair a cheerful holiday character with a line like “Merry Picklemas” or “Merry Spike-mas.”

Sporty people like gifts that come along to practice. A sticker fits a water bottle, gear bag or helmet, a hoodie is perfect for cold sidelines and early training, and a tee works for the team holiday party. Mugs suit the coach who runs on coffee, and a greeting card makes a kind end-of-season thank-you. Teams and clubs can pick one design for everyone as an easy group gift.

Check the size chart on the listing before ordering apparel, and collect sizes early for team orders. Everything is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order before the last practice before Christmas.
