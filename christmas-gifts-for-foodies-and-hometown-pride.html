More Christmas Gifts for Hobby Lovers: Foodies & Hometown Pride

This is the second half of our Christmas hobby guide, made for two kinds of people who are easy to love and surprisingly hard to shop for: the ones who live to eat, and the ones who never stop talking about where they come from. The food and drink section has something for sweet tooths and savory snackers alike, from cannoli, churros, shortbread, stollen and pavlova to brisket, gnocchi, shawarma, hummus and fondue, plus fruit fans who swear by figs, blueberries or persimmons. The pride sections celebrate home: US states from Alaska to Rhode Island, and heritage designs for families with roots in places like Brazil, Lebanon, Ethiopia, Croatia and the Philippines. Each pairs a soft holiday character with a pun like “Merry Cannoli-mas” or “Merry Tennessee-mas.”

Food lovers tend to keep their favorite gifts in the kitchen, so a mug for slow weekend breakfasts, a tote for market runs or a sticker on a well-loved recipe binder all get real use. Hometown designs shine on a hoodie for the trip back home, a sticker for the car or a card tucked into a care package for someone who moved away.

Check the size chart on the listing before ordering clothing. Every item is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order early in the holiday season.
