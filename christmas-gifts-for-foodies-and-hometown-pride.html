More Christmas Gifts for Hobby Lovers: Foodies & Food Lovers

This is part 2 of our Christmas hobby guide, made for people who live to eat and drink well. The food and drink section has something for sweet tooths and savory snackers alike, from cannoli, churros, shortbread, stollen and pavlova to brisket, gnocchi, shawarma, hummus and fondue, plus fruit fans who swear by figs, blueberries or persimmons, and drinkers who toast with cognac, limoncello, rum or bourbon. Each pairs a soft holiday character with a pun like “Merry Cannoli-mas” or “Merry Cognac-mas.”

Food lovers tend to keep their favorite gifts in the kitchen, so a mug for slow weekend breakfasts, a tote for market runs or a sticker on a well-loved recipe binder all get real use. A tee with their favorite treat is an easy win for anyone who cooks, bakes or hosts through the holidays.

Looking for hometown or heritage pride instead? Those designs now have their own third page, linked below. Check the size chart on the listing before ordering clothing. Every item is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order early in the holiday season.
