Christmas Gifts for Your Wife, Husband, Partner & In-Laws

Gifts for the people closest to you, and the family you gained along the way, deserve a little extra thought. This page collects Christmas designs for your wife, husband, boyfriend, girlfriend and fiancé or fiancée, for newlyweds celebrating their first married Christmas, and for every kind of in-law: mother-in-law, father-in-law, sister-in-law, brother-in-law, daughter-in-law and son-in-law. There are also picks for your best friend, your bougie bestie, your roommate and the neighbor who feels like family. Each pairs a soft holiday character with a sweet, slightly cheeky pun like “Merry Wifey-mas” or “Merry MIL-mas.”

For a partner, these work best as a playful extra next to a bigger present: a mug for slow holiday mornings, a cozy hoodie to borrow later, or a sticker for their laptop or water bottle. For in-laws, a design with their title is a warm welcome to a new family member’s first Christmas with you, and a card works on its own.

Check the size chart on the listing, or pick a mug, sticker or card if you are unsure. Each piece is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order early enough to wrap it.
