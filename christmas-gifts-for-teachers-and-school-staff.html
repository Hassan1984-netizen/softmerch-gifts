Christmas Gifts for Teachers & School Staff: Cute Pun Ideas

Christmas gifts for teachers work best when they feel personal without being fussy. This page is for the people who keep a school running: classroom teachers and homeschool parents, special education teams, school counselors, principals, school nurses, music, art and dance teachers, coaches, nannies, professors and bus drivers. Every design pairs a cheerful holiday character with a school-themed pun like “Merry Grammar-mas” or “Merry Little Elves,” so it reads as a real thank-you.

Teachers collect a lot of mugs, so think about what they will actually reach for. A sticker for a laptop or water bottle is a tiny, welcome extra, a tee or hoodie is great for spirit days and winter field trips, and a greeting card with a handwritten note from your child often means the most. Room parents can also choose one design and give it from the whole class.

If you are buying clothing, check the size chart on the listing, or stick with a sticker, mug or card when in doubt. Items are printed to order, so look at Redbubble’s delivery estimates and order well before the last week of school ahead of winter break, leaving time to wrap and add a note.
