Christmas Gifts for Sisters, Brothers, Aunts, Uncles & Cousins

The extended family list is where gift ideas usually run out. These Christmas designs give every relative their own pun: big and little sisters, brothers, the fun auntie, the uncle with the dad jokes, cousins, nieces, nephews, granddaughters, grandsons, godparents and even the only child who never had to share. Each pairs a cheerful holiday character with a line like “Merry Auntie-mas” or “Merry Cousin-mas.”

These designs shine in groups. Matching tees or hoodies make a great family photo, a cousins’ gift swap is easy when everyone gets their own sticker, and a mug is a safe pick for the relative you only see once a year. Stickers suit water bottles, laptops and school folders, which makes them handy for nieces and nephews, and a greeting card with their design is a sweet extra for grandkids.

For clothing, check the size chart on the listing, and collect sizes early if you are ordering matching shirts for a group. Every item is printed to order, so check Redbubble’s delivery estimates and place group orders well before Christmas.
